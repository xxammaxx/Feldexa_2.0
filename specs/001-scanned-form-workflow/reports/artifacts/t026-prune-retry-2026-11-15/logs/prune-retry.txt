T026 review of 0785e5c - no-resurrection retry

Finding:
  After a PRUNE_FSYNC failure the history stayed complete but the file was
  already missing from the namespace. A retry that deleted nothing skipped
  the directory fsync, filtered the missing entry and trimmed the head. The
  save-side history rebuild also dropped missing entries before prune. A
  no-resurrection retry could therefore forget a non-durable delete.

Fix in 57ce38b:
  * buildHeadHistory retains every previous entry; only the pruner drops a
    missing entry after durability confirmation;
  * prune performs the revision-directory fsync when it deleted anything OR
    when any known history entry is missing;
  * fsync failure or a missing directory returns the full history with
    deletesDurable=false and no durable-prune count;
  * head, keep set, unknown-kind entries, and untracked legacy files stay
    protected.

Tests:
  * repeated fsync failure without resurrection: first retry still fails and
    keeps the entry; the next successful retry fsyncs and only then trims;
  * next save retains the unconfirmed-missing entry and trims after the
    prune's confirmed fsync;
  * device variant of the repeated no-resurrection retry.
