T026 security review of ff8f7fb - prune delete durability

Finding:
  pruneDocumentFromHead swallowed a fsyncDirectory failure after deleting
  known non-kept revisions, then filtered history by visible files. Save and
  startup wrote that shortened history. After a crash, a non-durable delete
  could reappear as an untracked file and bypass retention forever.

Fix in 0c75c57:
  * new StoreFaultPoint.PRUNE_FSYNC before the revision-directory fsync;
  * if that fsync fails, return the complete head history and
    deletesDurable=false; do not trim, do not count a durable prune;
  * save/startup keep the full history and retry on the next startup/save;
  * verified head, policy keep set, unknown-kind entries, and untracked
    legacy files remain protected.

Tests:
  * save-path fsync failure + crash resurrection of rev1; history stays full;
    next startup deletes rev1 and only then trims;
  * startup-path fsync failure + crash resurrection; report count stays 0;
    next startup retries and trims;
  * device variant on the real AndroidKeyStore.
