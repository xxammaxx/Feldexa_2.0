T026 owner decision - bounded head history and automatic retention

Head field:
  history: [{revisionId, kind}, ...]
  * oldest first, at most 16 entries;
  * kind = autosave | explicit | recovery | null (legacy/unknown);
  * encoded atomically inside the encrypted head.fx1;
  * built from the previous head history (existing files only), the previous
    head's own {revisionId, kind}, and the new revision;
  * a legacy head without history is valid and prunes nothing.

Applied after the durable step-7 read-back:
  keep = last 3 known autosaves + newest known explicit + newest known recovery
         + verified head
  delete only known-kind revision files not in keep;
  never delete the verified head, unknown-kind entries, or untracked legacy
  files;
  then atomically rewrite head.fx1 with surviving history entries.

Crash/restart:
  a crash between the durable head and the prune is reconciled on startup or
  the next save; a crash between deletion and history trim is reconciled by
  filtering missing entries. A failed delete is retried on the next commit.
  Deletions happen only after the head/revision read-back verification.

Races:
  the per-aggregate JVM lock wraps commit + prune; it does not protect
  cross-process writers.

Tests:
  seven head-history tests cover ordering, policy, unknown kinds, legacy
  heads, crash reconciliation, the 16-entry bound, and delete failure;
  three device tests cover the policy, legacy head, and unknown entry on the
  real AndroidKeyStore.
