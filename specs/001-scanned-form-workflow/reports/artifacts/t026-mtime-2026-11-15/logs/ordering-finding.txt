T026 security review finding - revision-order mtime durability

Review of 3197ca3: EncryptedStore setLastModified after the writer's
FileChannel.force and before the atomic rename; only the parent directory was
fsynced afterwards. The durability of the changed inode mtime was therefore
not proven.

Correction in 53ffe78:
  setLastModified(staged file)
  -> StoreIo.fsyncFile(staged file)          # flush inode metadata
  -> atomic rename to rev/<id>.fx1
  -> fsync(rev directory)

A new ORDERING_FSYNC fault point sits before the metadata fsync. Two
deterministic host tests record the actual order and prove that an injected
metadata-fsync failure prevents the rename, keeps the old head, and removes the
staged file.

Limits:
  * A JVM/unit test cannot simulate a physical power loss. The claim is limited
    to the syscall order and the fail-closed behavior on an injected error.
  * The per-aggregate JVM lock serializes this process only; cross-process
    writers are not claimed.
  * Retention (section 3.1 step 8) is unchanged and remains with the owner's
    pending policy decision; this correction makes no format decision.
