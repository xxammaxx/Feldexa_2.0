------------------------------------------

freshly generated evidence bundle was still UNTRACKED/MODIFIED in the worktree,
i.e. before the evidence commit. `git diff --check` inspects the unstaged
differences, so it reported the trailing spaces that the device itself prints on

the historical log was intentionally NOT rewritten afterwards.
