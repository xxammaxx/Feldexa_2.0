## 6. What `PageGeometry` guarantees

### 6.1 Display box

- `mediaBox` is the full PDF page box; `cropBox` is the requested CropBox
  intersected with the MediaBox, and it defaults to the MediaBox when absent.
  The intersection is recomputed on construction and on JSON read, so
  `cx0 == cy0 == 0` is never assumed.
- A CropBox that does not overlap the MediaBox in a positive-area rectangle is
  rejected with `ArgumentError`.
- `displaySize` is `(cropWidth, cropHeight)` for rotation 0/180 and
  `(cropHeight, cropWidth)` for rotation 90/270. `displayBox` is
  `[0, 0, displayWidth, displayHeight]`.
- The only supported rotations are exactly `0`, `90`, `180`, and `270`; any
  other integer is rejected.

### 6.2 Mapping

- `displayToPdf` and `pdfToDisplay` implement the four §2 point formulas
  exactly. PDF rectangles are represented as lower-left plus width/height;
  display rectangles as top-left plus width/height.
- `displayRectToPdf` and `pdfRectToDisplay` map the two opposite corners and
  return their bounding rectangle, so width and height swap for rotations 90
  and 270 and the operations are exact inverses.
- Crop offsets are applied through the effective crop box on every side; tests
  include `(10, 20)` offsets, a CropBox clipped against a smaller MediaBox, and
  a negative-origin MediaBox.

### 6.3 Validation and serialization

- `validate` implements contract §6.1: an overshoot of at most `0.5` pt is
  clamped once and reported through `GeometryValidation.wasClamped`, while a
  larger overshoot is rejected with `ArgumentError`. Each edge is clamped into
  the display box; a rectangle that lies entirely outside within tolerance is
  translated to the nearest edge so it keeps its positive size. The returned
  rectangle is exactly inside the display box. Clamping is never silent.
- `toJson` persists `mediaBox`, `cropBox`, and `rotation`; `displaySize` is
  derived and is deliberately not persisted, so it cannot disagree with the
  boxes. `fromJson` recomputes the crop intersection and rejects malformed
  input with `FormatException`.

### 6.5 Contract note

The section 2 prose for rotation 180 says "Display top-left is the PDF
top-right", but the same section's normative formula maps display `(0, 0)` to
PDF `(cx1, cy0)`, which is the PDF bottom-right corner. The formula, the §2
verification table, and a 180-degree rotation of the rotation-0 mapping all
agree with each other. This implementation follows the normative formula and
table; the prose label is the outlier.
