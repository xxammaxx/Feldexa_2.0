### 5.2 Independent-review correction

The independent review of candidate `85f98ca` reported that
`PageGeometry.validate` on `mediaBox = (0, 0, 0.1, 10)` and
`rect = (-0.4, 1, 0.2, 1)` returned a rectangle with `right = 0.2` even though
`displayWidth = 0.1`. The failure was reproduced on the exact published bundle
(`.dart_tool/t016-merge-candidate.bundle`, head `85f98ca`): the returned
rectangle was `(0, 1, 0.2, 1)` with `wasClamped = true`, so a stored rectangle
violated contract §6.1's invariant.

**Root cause.** `validate` clamps each edge into the display box. When an edge
pair collapses, it translates a rectangle that lies entirely outside back to
the nearest edge so the positive size is not lost. That translation preserved
the original width and height unconditionally. The 0.5 pt overshoot bound limits
an entirely outside rectangle to at most 0.5 pt in size, but a display
dimension may itself be smaller than 0.5 pt (the reported page is 0.1 pt wide).
In that case the translated far edge lands outside the display box: a 0.2 pt
wide rectangle moved to the left edge of a 0.1 pt wide page ends at `right =
0.2`. The same defect applies to the right, top, and bottom edges, to corners,
and to any rotation whose display width or height is below the rectangle size.

**Fix.** The translation branches cap the kept size at the display dimension
(`min(rect.width, displayWidth)` and `min(rect.height, displayHeight)`) before
pinning the far edge. The cap moves an edge only by an amount already inside the
0.5 pt tolerance, preserves the current behavior for normal PDF page sizes
(where the display dimension exceeds any tolerated overshoot), and cannot
produce a rectangle outside the display box. Overshoots beyond 0.5 pt are still
rejected, and clamping is still reported through `wasClamped`.

**Regression coverage.** The suite now pins the exact review case; the same
regression on all four edges; both narrow width and narrow height on rotations
0, 90, 180, and 270; all four tiny-square corner outliers; near-zero display
dimensions (`1e-9` and `1e-12` pt); extreme display aspect ratios
(`1e-4 × 1e6` and `1e6 × 1e-4`); and a scale matrix from `1e-12` to `595` pt
that asserts every accepted result stays inside the display box. The normal A4
translation behavior is pinned separately so the cap does not regress ordinary
pages.

**Assessment boundary.** A retained sweep
(`.dart_tool/t016-logs/fix-final/invariant-sweep.dart`, output
`12-invariant-sweep.log`) ran 212,992 targeted cases across all four rotations,
display dimensions from `1e-12` to `1e6` pt, and every edge and corner, plus a
30,000-case seeded fuzz — 242,992 cases in total. After the fix it found zero
accepted rectangles outside the display box, zero accepted overshoots above
0.5 pt, and zero non-degenerate false rejections. A numerically degenerate
rectangle whose edge difference is below double precision (its stored width or
height is positive but its computed right or bottom equals its left or top) can
still be rejected with the existing "cannot be clamped ... without changing its
size" `ArgumentError` instead of being stored. That is an honest rejection, not
an out-of-bounds return, and it predates this correction.

## 6. What `PageGeometry` guarantees

### 6.1 Display box

- `mediaBox` is the full PDF page box; `cropBox` is the requested CropBox
  intersected with the MediaBox, and it defaults to the MediaBox when absent.
  The intersection is recomputed on construction and on JSON read, so
  `cx0 == cy0 == 0` is never assumed.
- A CropBox that does not overlap the MediaBox in a positive-area rectangle is
  rejected with `ArgumentError`.
- `displaySize` is `(cropWidth, cropHeight)` for rotation 0/180 and
  `(cropHeight, cropWidth)` for rotation 90/270. `displayBox` is
  `[0, 0, displayWidth, displayHeight]`.
- The only supported rotations are exactly `0`, `90`, `180`, and `270`; any
  other integer is rejected.

### 6.2 Mapping

- `displayToPdf` and `pdfToDisplay` implement the four §2 point formulas
  exactly. PDF rectangles are represented as lower-left plus width/height;
  display rectangles as top-left plus width/height.
- `displayRectToPdf` and `pdfRectToDisplay` map the two opposite corners and
  return their bounding rectangle, so width and height swap for rotations 90
  and 270 and the operations are exact inverses.
- Crop offsets are applied through the effective crop box on every side; tests
  include `(10, 20)` offsets, a CropBox clipped against a smaller MediaBox, and
  a negative-origin MediaBox.

### 6.3 Validation and serialization

- `validate` implements contract §6.1: an overshoot of at most `0.5` pt is
  clamped once and reported through `GeometryValidation.wasClamped`, while a
  larger overshoot is rejected with `ArgumentError`. Each edge is clamped into
  the display box; a rectangle that lies entirely outside within tolerance is
  translated to the nearest edge so it keeps its positive size, capped at the
  display dimension so a page narrower or shorter than the rectangle cannot push
  the far edge outside. The returned rectangle is exactly inside the display
  box for every rotation and every display size. Clamping is never silent.
- `toJson` persists `mediaBox`, `cropBox`, and `rotation`; `displaySize` is
  derived and is deliberately not persisted, so it cannot disagree with the
  boxes. `fromJson` recomputes the crop intersection and rejects malformed
  input with `FormatException`.

The section 2 prose for rotation 180 says "Display top-left is the PDF
top-right", but the same section's normative formula maps display `(0, 0)` to
PDF `(cx1, cy0)`, which is the PDF bottom-right corner. The formula, the §2

## 11. Limits

- T016 is the page-geometry task only. Zoom/view transforms, hit-testing,
  overlay painting, recognition raster mapping, export CTMs, and the broader
  property suite at multiple zoom factors remain with T070, T069, T078, T104,
  and later tasks.
- `PageGeometry` validates against the display box. It does not itself enforce
  a minimum field size; the 4 pt resize floor and hit-area inflation are UI
  concerns.
- `displaySize` is derived and is not persisted. A reader always recomputes it
  from the boxes and rotation.
- The 0.5 pt clamp/reject threshold is the contract value. Clamping is reported
  through `wasClamped`; callers that persist a clamped rectangle must record a
  warning at their own boundary.
- The `gradlew.bat` line-ending artifact pre-dates this task and is untouched by
  the merge; it is neither staged nor committed.
- The final candidate builds on the previously reviewed merge commit `85f98ca`
  and changes only `page_geometry.dart`, `geometry_test.dart`, and this report.
  The T032/T035 files and reports are inherited unchanged from integrated main.
  Running `validate_report.dart` directly against those historical reports on
  the merged tree reports binding mismatches because each report intentionally
  binds its own artifact rows and dirty-file hashes to its original candidate
  revision; that is expected historical binding, not a T016 or merge regression,
  and it is not claimed as a merged-tree PASS. The T032/T035 host suites and the
  shared schema/validator regression suite do pass on the merged tree (§7).
- The narrow-page correction normalizes edge overshoots; it does not claim that
  an arbitrarily sized rectangle is meaningful for a page smaller than the
  rectangle. A numerically degenerate rectangle whose stored width or height is
  positive but whose computed far edge equals its near edge (a difference below
  double precision) can still be rejected with `ArgumentError` rather than
  silently resized; it is never returned outside the display box.
- No `SIMULATED` browser result is claimed for T016, and no `EMULATOR` or
  `DEVICE` result was produced. T016 cannot prove user-visible alignment
  (SC-017), export placement, or Android behavior on its own.
- The reviewer-owned `precision-testability.md` checklist remains unchecked;
  this report claims no requirements-quality approval. Independent review,
  integration, post-merge verification, and convergence remain external gates.
