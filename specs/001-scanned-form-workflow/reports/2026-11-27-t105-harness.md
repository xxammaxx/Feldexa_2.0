## Delivered

`tools/pdf_verify/verify_artifact.py` verifies the executable rows of
`contracts/export-plan.md` §6 against one artifact and its source:

- page count with a page-size order proxy — PASS/FAIL (uniform pages cannot prove order),
- per-page size and rotation equality within 0.01 pt from `pdfinfo` — PASS/FAIL,
- `/AcroForm` and `/Widget` byte scan plus `pdfinfo Form:` — PASS/FAIL (byte scan is not a structural parse),
- text extraction plus German glyph presence, optional expected values — PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL,
- 150 dpi render of page 1; 300 dpi renders with Poppler and, when present, MuPDF — PARTIAL without MuPDF,
- source SHA-256 against `--source-sha256`,
- explicit `NOT_RUN` rows whose reason is "not implemented": masked appearance,
  no-clipping measurement, checkbox/signature rendered state; placement is
  `NOT_RUN` without an expectation; artifact isolation is task-owned.

```
python3 tools/pdf_verify/verify_artifact.py \
  --source tools/corpus/synthetic/sf-001.pdf \
  --artifact specs/001-scanned-form-workflow/reports/artifacts/2026-11-26-t344-export-proof-depth/export-artifact-1.pdf \
  --source-sha256 "$(sha256sum tools/corpus/synthetic/sf-001.pdf | cut -d' ' -f1)"
```
