## 1. Why this final report exists

Review B blocked the previous evidence because `final-artifacts-final.txt` and
the build log recorded `HEAD=3a3202c` while the T027 code was still the
uncommitted working-tree change that became `5b4c295`; the report therefore did
not prove that the committed candidate source produced the APKs. Review A then
found three concrete defects in the `4ddbe7c` candidate:

1. `EncryptedStore.writeSignature` could replace an existing immutable
   signature asset through `atomicMove(REPLACE_EXISTING)`;
2. `AndroidPdfrxRasterClient.evict` removed page-size entries for every source
   sharing the adapter id;
3. the Kotlin canonical-JSON double formatter used JVM `Double.toString` rather
   than Dart's canonical spelling, and the native JSON parser did not accept
   exponent numbers at all.

The correction is committed **before** the final build at `d45f4df`. The
current report and its artifacts are in a **later docs-only commit** that adds
evidence and report indexing; they do not rerun or rebrand the build. The
candidate code/tree named above is the exact tree that was built and tested.
