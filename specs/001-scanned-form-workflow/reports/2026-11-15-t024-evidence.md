## 1. Scope

This report covers **T024 / issue #24 only**: the single `FeldexaPlugin`
MethodChannel/EventChannel facade in
`apps/feldexa_app/android/app/src/main/kotlin/de/feldexa/app/FeldexaPlugin.kt`,
its registration from `MainActivity.kt`, the test-only JUnit dependency, and the
host-JVM contract tests in
`apps/feldexa_app/android/app/src/test/kotlin/de/feldexa/app/FeldexaPluginTest.kt`.

The facade owns channel registration, strict payload validation, the success
envelope, catalog-coded `PlatformException` errors, bounded background dispatch,
operation cancellation, event publishing/coalescing, and safe detach/destruction.
It deliberately implements **no** import, PDF, OCR, storage, export, or delivery
capability. Those remain T025/T026/T038–T040; until a service prefix is registered
through `FeldexaPlugin.registerService`, its methods fail closed with `PLT-001`.
Consequently T024 structurally enables the FR-001/FR-013/FR-095 platform calls but
does not claim those capabilities are implemented.

`MainActivity` was changed because `FeldexaPlugin` is application Kotlin, not a
published plugin package, so GeneratedPluginRegistrant cannot discover it. The
activity adds the plugin, registers `de.feldexa/import` as required by §1, and
forwards `resumed`/`inactive`/`paused`/`detached` lifecycle events. Kotlin does not
autosave (§9). The `testImplementation("junit:junit:4.13.2")` line is test-only and
changes no runtime dependency or production APK behavior.

The built-in `checklists/requirements.md` is fully checked (16/16). The custom
`checklists/precision-testability.md` remains reviewer-owned with its 36 intentionally

### 5.1 Contract interpretations recorded for review

- `contracts/platform-channel.md` §1 says payloads carry `schemaVersion`; §4/§5/§6/§7 tables often abbreviate it away. T024 requires `schemaVersion == 1` for every method payload, including `store.status` and `cancel.operation`, so version drift fails loudly instead of defaulting.
- §8 defines event maps without a version envelope; §6 shows an `event` discriminator. T024 events follow §8's fields plus `event` and intentionally do not carry the method success envelope.
- §6 names export progress `export.progress` while §8 names the generic event `operation.progress`. T024 supports both typed helpers (`OperationContext.exportProgress` and `OperationContext.progress`) rather than dropping either contract statement.
- §10's revision cap is applied to the `revision` map at `store.saveRevision` and `store.createTemplate`; the signature cap maps to `SAVE-007`, the plan cap and total payload cap to `PLT-003`.
