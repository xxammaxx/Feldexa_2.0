## 1. Scope

Two findings from the two independent reviews of candidate `afac5b8f`:

- **F1 (MAJOR) — deadline safety of recovery head commits.** `startupRecovery`
  checked expiry before entering `pruneDocumentFromHead`, but that method then
  deleted revisions and the shortened head was committed without a fresh check;
  the fallback path in `recoverAggregate` could also commit a head after the
  deadline expired during verification. The existing deadline is now threaded
  through `recoverAggregate`, `findNewestVerifiableRevision`, and
  `pruneDocumentFromHead`, and is re-checked immediately before every affected
  durable head commit; once expired, no new head is created/committed and no
  later destructive work starts. With no deadline supplied, behavior is
  unchanged (every added check is gated by `deadlineEpochMs != null`).
- **F2 (MINOR) — exact deadline range validation.** `LongRule` checked a
  floating-point value's integrality, converted with `Double.toLong()`, and
  only then applied bounds. A finite out-of-range value such as `1e20`
  saturates to `Long.MAX_VALUE` and was accepted because the max is
  `Long.MAX_VALUE`. Range is now validated on the exact numeric value *before*
  conversion, so `1e20` and the rounded `2^63` (`Long.MAX_VALUE.toDouble()`)
  are rejected while a genuine `Long.MAX_VALUE`, `Long.MAX_VALUE - 1023`
  (`2^63 - 1024`), and their valid boundaries are accepted.

## 4. Full-suite failure analysis (pre-existing, not a regression)

`EncryptedStoreReviewTest.eightyMiBObjectVerifiesUnder128MiBHeap` spawns a child
JVM (`-Xmx128m`) running `MemoryBoundedStoreProbe`, which commits an 80 MiB
object. It failed with `StorageWriteException(SAVE-003)` from
`mapWriteFailure → writeContainer → stageObject → commitObject →
commitObjectForTest → MemoryBoundedStoreProbe.main`. `mapWriteFailure`
rethrows `Error` (so this was not an OOM) and maps a non-quota write throwable
to `SAVE-003`; the cause is environmental in the memory-bounded subprocess.

Causality check: the probe exercises `commitObjectForTest → commitObject →
stageObject → writeContainer`; round 6 changed none of those. Every deadline
check added is gated by `deadlineEpochMs != null` and defaults to `null`, so the
no-deadline large-object path is logically identical. Empirical control:
restoring the exact reviewed baseline `EncryptedStore.kt` (`afac5b8f`, sha
`765b53f0…`) and re-running the isolated test produced the **identical**
failure (only source line numbers differ); my file was then restored and its
sha re-verified. No fix was applied — this is out of scope and pre-existing at
