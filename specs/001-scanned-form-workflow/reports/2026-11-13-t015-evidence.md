## 5. What the value objects guarantee

### 5.1 `Point`

- Immutable `x`/`y` doubles in page display-space PDF points.
- Rejects NaN, positive infinity, and negative infinity at construction.
- Accepts zero, negative, subnormal, and maximum finite coordinates; negative
  values matter for CropBox offsets and for values validated later.
- `Point.zero` is a compile-time constant; equality and `hashCode` use both
  coordinates.

### 5.2 `Size`

- Immutable `width`/`height` doubles in points.
- Rejects NaN, infinities, zero, negative zero, and negative dimensions.
- Accepts any strictly positive finite size, including a 6 pt checkbox; the
  model deliberately imposes no minimum interactive size.
- Exposes `aspectRatio`; equality and `hashCode` use both dimensions.

### 5.3 `Rect`

- Immutable `left`, `top`, `width`, `height` with `x`/`y` aliases matching the
  contract's JSON field names.
- Rejects non-finite origins, non-positive or non-finite dimensions, and finite
  operands whose right/bottom edge would overflow to infinity. `right`,
  `bottom`, `origin`, and `size` are therefore always finite and valid.
- Accepts a 6 pt checkbox (`Rect.fromLTWH(100, 50, 6, 6)`) and negative finite
  origins, consistent with CropBox offsets.
- Full double precision is retained in memory. Exact equality compares all four
  fields; no epsilon tolerance is hidden in the value object.

### 5.4 Serialization

`toJson()` quantizes to three decimals as required by `data-model.md` §2.2 and
uses the contract field names (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`; `width`, `height`
for `Size`; `x`, `y` for `Point`). Stored values are not mutated. A positive
dimension that would quantize to zero is written as `0.001` pt, so every valid
value object survives a JSON round-trip without violating the positive-size
invariant. `fromJson()` rejects missing, non-numeric, non-finite, and
non-positive fields with `FormatException`; constructors use `ArgumentError`.
Canonical key ordering remains the responsibility of the T017 canonical JSON
encoder.

### 5.5 Traceability

| Source | Satisfied by |
| --- | --- |
| `data-model.md` §2.2 | Finite coordinates; `width > 0` and `height > 0`; 3-decimal serialization only; small 6 pt rectangles accepted |
| `contracts/coordinates-and-geometry.md` §1–§2 | Display-space PDF points, top-left origin, y down; negative crop offsets representable; no device-pixel truth |
