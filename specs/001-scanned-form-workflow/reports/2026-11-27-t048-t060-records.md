conformance fix described in §2.3). It runs two independent trials

1. **Byte identity and unchanged modification time of the picked file** —

   assertions are lines 307–313, and the delete plus two empty-directory
   assertions are lines 356–360.

   `AndroidStoreClient.loadDocument` plus an explicit `store.readSource`
   channel read return the protected bytes (reopen while moved at lines
   315–326, after delete at line 367); PDFium renders every page and the
   digests match the fixture reference. The export clause is probed

   branch records `PLT-001`/`platform.unsupported` and asserts that no export
