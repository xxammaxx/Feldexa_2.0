## 1. Scope and question

Nine ledger rows in `specs/001-scanned-form-workflow/tasks.md` are still open although
their slice PRs are integrated. Several carry long progress and limit notes; the T086 row
carries no note at all even though its evidence report was later rewritten from `FAIL` to
`PASS`. This report answers one question per row:

> What does the row literally demand, what is integrated on `origin/main`, which committed
> evidence exists, and is that enough to tick the row — or what exactly is missing?

The answer is not an approval. A `CLOSEABLE` verdict means the row text is satisfied by
integrated, committed evidence and names the tick text the owner/parent can apply. A
`STILL OPEN` verdict names the exact remainder; owner decisions are never taken here.

- `EMULATOR` is not `DEVICE`; an emulator timing is never SC-004.

- A progress note that says `FAIL`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED` or `Not started` is respected
  unless later committed evidence supersedes it.
