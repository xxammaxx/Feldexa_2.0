  IoU ≥ 0.5), the preregistered fair-comparison controls, the benchmark-integrity

  no expected result.
- **Quality-review correction: PASS.** The earlier `v1.0.0` §6.4 accepted
  `R_pooled`/`U_pooled` and merely disclosed a below-80% form, so an individual
  form could fail while the run passed. SC-003 says "the share of a form's
  fields" and D-18 says to compare "for each corpus form"; neither defines a
  pooled threshold. `v1.1.0` requires both `R_f ≥ 0.80` and `U_f ≥ 0.80` for
  every form, makes any single failing form `FAIL` when the measurement is
  valid, and demotes pooled values to descriptive context. The old candidate was
  reproduced with seven violations; the revision has zero.
- **T032 integration: PASS for the merge mechanics.** The merge commit has
  parents `0b5a0a5` and `a34456f`; the merge base is `4932af4`. The README
  conflict was resolved with both the T032 and T035 rows, and `tasks.md` contains
