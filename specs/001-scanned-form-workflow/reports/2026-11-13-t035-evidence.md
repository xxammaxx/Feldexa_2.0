## 5. What the protocol records

`tools/evaluation/manual-baseline.md` is a normative method, not a result. It
contains no measured reduction, no expected value, and no placeholder presented
as data. Its sections define:

- **Normative sources and precedence** for SC-003, D-18, the frozen corpus and
  metric, and the constitution.
- **Terms and counting rules**: the four D-18 operation classes add, move,
  resize, and reclassify; net per-field/property counting; separate secondary
  counts for confirm, reject, remove, reorder, navigation, and committed
  operations; the undo/redo rule and the no-epsilon rule.
- **Equal conditions**: same operator, same tool/build, same authoritative
  Android device/profile, same PDF bytes, same ground truth and metric, same
  instructions, same zoom/pan policy, no values, offline, and same measurement
  schema.
- **Fair-comparison controls**: operator qualification and GT independence,
  preregistration and freezing, balanced condition order, at least 24-hour
  washout, a strict quality-parity gate (TP = GT count, FP = 0, FN = 0, every
  match same-type with IoU ≥ 0.5), required action-log instrumentation,
  condition blinding, and a no-tuning rule.
- **Execution and rerun rules**: precondition checks, per-form sequence,
  scoring sequence, and the rule that invalid or failed runs are retained and
  replaced only by a new separately reported run.
- **Metrics**: per-form `M_f`, `D_f`, `R_f`, untouched fields, `U_f`, and pooled
  `R_pooled`/`U_pooled` computed from totals. SC-003 requires both
  `R_pooled ≥ 0.80` and `U_pooled ≥ 0.80`, with the per-form distribution
  reported and no form excluded or substituted.
- **Required T142 record**: run identity, per-form table, pooled summary, and
  evidence/limitation fields.
- **Prohibited practices**: a benchmark-integrity table covering detection in
  the manual condition, GT access, per-form tuning, order changes, early
  stopping, form exclusion, rule changes, hidden work, selective reporting,
  class mixing, and post-hoc re-scoring.
- **Reproducibility, limitations, versioning, compliance map, and a no-results
  statement.**

The protocol depends on artifacts that T033/T034/T142 have not yet produced
(`corpus-1.0.0`, `metric-definition.v1.md`, the scorer, and the T142 report).
They are named as frozen/later inputs, not as existing measurements. That is
why T035 is independent of T034 and why no SC-003 value can appear here.
