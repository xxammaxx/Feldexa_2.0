**Purpose**: Assess whether the existing specification and plan state requirements with enough precision to be measured and accepted, focusing on ambiguity, measurable acceptance criteria, and cross-artifact consistency. This checklist does not reopen the five settled clarifications or the ratified constitution; it treats them as fixed inputs and asks only how their consequences are operationalized.
**Created**: 2026-11-12
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md) | [plan.md](../plan.md)

**Focus areas**: Requirement clarity and ambiguity; acceptance-criteria measurability; cross-artifact consistency and traceability; scenario and edge-case coverage where it affects testability.

**Audience / timing**: Reviewer; requirements gate before `speckit-tasks`.
**Fixed inputs**: The five settled clarifications and constitution v1.0.0 are not reopened; only their operational precision is assessed.
